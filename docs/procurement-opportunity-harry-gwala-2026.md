## Chatbliz OS employee workflow
1. **Research/Hunting**: retrieve permitted official tender documentation; record reference, source URL, publication/closing dates, amendment history and buyer identity. Respect site access rules.
2. **Fishing/Qualification**: assess eligibility, prior references, required financials, tax status, supplier registrations, capacity to buy media and deliver ads, costs and commercial viability. Record unknowns; no fabricated qualifications.
3. **Mentor/Adviser/Legal Support**: identify contractual risk, submission rules and compliance gaps. Escalate uncertain eligibility to the owner.
4. **Design Influence**: prepare an advertising portfolio and example layouts only from authorized assets.
5. **Studio/Content Creator**: prepare an optional pitch/video proof of capability; no publication without owner approval.
6. **Closer/Negotiator**: prepare an approved bid or enquiry; use official procurement channels and deadlines. Do not represent an invitation or appointment unless accepted.
7. **Fixer/Business Analyst**: monitor tasks, deadlines, app errors, consent and payment integrity; report verified outcomes separately.
