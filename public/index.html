HOW CHATBLIZ WORKS

From your brand to your business

One practical business journey

Start with what customers should know about you. Carry that promise into your online experience and content, then use customer response to choose the next business action.

  1. 01

    GET CLEAR

    My Brand

    Define who you serve, what problem you solve and why customers should choose you. Turn it into a clear message and visibility plan.

    Output: a clear customer promise
  2. 02

    MAKE IT EASY TO ACT

    My Website & App

    Use the customer promise to plan useful pages, app screens and a clear next step, such as asking for a quote or contacting you.

    Output: a site or app brief
  3. 03

    SHOW UP CONSISTENTLY

    My Studio

    Plan posts, video, podcast or campaign ideas that repeat the same customer promise and invite the right people to take action.

    Output: a content plan
  4. 04

    LEARN AND IMPROVE

    My Business

    Track enquiries, sales, follow-ups and tasks. Use what customers do and say to improve the offer, brand and next campaign.

    Output: a measurable next action

What works today: your account can save your business profile, conversations and tasks. Chatbliz creates planning guidance in the app. Website building, brand search, content production or publishing, lead capture, WhatsApp, bookings and payments still need their service connections before they can happen here.

MY WEBSITE & APP · DEVELOPER INTERFACE

Build for each customer

One sign-in · separate project memory

Sign in once, create a separate build brief for each customer business, and use the same four-workspace loop to shape its brand, website or app, content and operations. Save customer feedback as a project lesson; Chatbliz can include that lesson in the next build prompt.

Customer builds

Loading projects

Learning and privacy: saved lessons are owner-entered project notes, reused only when you ask Chatbliz to continue that same project. This does not train the underlying AI model or share one customer's notes with another. Keep passwords, payment details and private customer information out of build notes. Code generation, customer logins and live publishing still require connected developer and hosting services.
